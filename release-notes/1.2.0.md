Modes that count the day in sessions, gentler break suggestions, and a day off.

- Modes are now Chill, Moderate and Hardcore: 10, 15 and 20 sessions of 25 minutes with a 5 minute break. Moderate fills a 7.5 hour working day, with lunch on top. Change the numbers in Settings, Modes
- The daily rest target is gone. Rest is due in the rhythm of the mode, 5 minutes for every 25 of focus, and energy follows that balance
- Settings from earlier versions carry over: a focus goal in hours becomes the matching number of sessions
- New: after an hour of focus without a break the window that ends a session suggests a coffee and puts the break buttons first
- New: after half the day's sessions, or four hours of focus, it suggests a long break. How long is up to you
- New: time at the Mac. One line at the bottom of the timer window shows how long you were at the keyboard or mouse today next to the focus you logged, and how much of it had no timer. Statistics has the details and a row for each day of the week. Only the fact of input is counted, on this Mac
- Rest that is due is counted in whole breaks, one for each session done
- Energy takes the place of the break bar in the timer window: the mood, the level, and under it the rest you took against the rest that is due
- The energy bar changes colour: green from 50%, orange from 25%, red below
- The advice line starts with a small picture of what to do next, a coffee, a walk, a stop sign
- The widget matches the app: the same colours, the same advice line, and the same mark in the ring, a bolt in focus, a cup on a break, play when nothing runs
- New: Day off mode. No goal, no reminders, nothing sent to the phone. It ends by itself at midnight
- Phone notifications are now about one thing, being away from the Mac. The buttons are +5, +15 and +30 minutes of rest from now, and the time already spent away counts as rest. Focus is started at the Mac

**Install:** nothing to do if you have 1.1.0, the app updates itself. From an older version, download `Focus-Energy.pkg` and run it over the old one.
