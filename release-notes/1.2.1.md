A simpler "Break is over" window, and a fix for a session that waited overnight.

- The window that ends a break is shorter: one button to start the next focus session, a row to rest a little longer, and a small status line with sessions, energy and rest
- The Stop button and the row of focus lengths are gone from that window. Closing it means "not now": the timer stops and nothing starts
- Fixed: a finished session left unanswered overnight was shown as focus done the next day. It is now closed at midnight and logged on the day it happened

**Install:** nothing to do if you have 1.1.0 or later, the app updates itself. From an older version, download `Focus-Energy.pkg` and run it over the old one.
