Fixes for the desktop widget.

- After midnight the widget starts the new day from zero by itself, instead of showing yesterday until the app next writes to it
- The widget now reads its data again on a schedule, so it catches up even when macOS ignores the app's request to refresh it
- Installing an update now refreshes the widgets straight away
- The line under the countdown ("extend or rest", "break over") no longer spills out of the ring

**Install:** download `Focus-Energy.pkg` and run it over the old version. Your log and settings stay.
