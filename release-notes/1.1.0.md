A nudge when you forget to start the timer, and updates that install themselves.

- New: if you have been at the keyboard or mouse for ten minutes with no timer running, a notification offers to start a focus session, or to start one and count the time so far
- The delay is adjustable from 5 to 60 minutes, and the reminder can be switched off, in Settings, Notifications
- The reminder repeats every 15 minutes while you keep working, pauses when you walk away, and stops for the day once the daily focus goal is reached
- New: the app updates itself. It looks for a new version once a day and installs it with one click, or automatically if you switch that on in Settings, General. Updates are accepted only with the Home App Labs signature
- Buttons respond to the pointer: the round time and break buttons lift and brighten on hover
- The labels above the button rows are a little larger

**Install:** download `Focus-Energy.pkg` and run it over the old version. Your log and settings stay. This is the last update you have to install by hand.
