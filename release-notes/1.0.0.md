The first public version of Focus Energy.

- Focus timer in the menu bar, the Dock and a window, with sessions you can extend instead of restarting
- Breaks with their own buttons and a daily rest target
- Energy bar, day ratings and a weekly overview
- Light, Moderate and Hardcore modes
- Desktop widgets in three sizes
- Optional phone notifications with answer buttons through the ntfy app
- Time log with after-the-fact entries, optional storage in iCloud Drive

**Install:** download `Focus-Energy.pkg` and open it. macOS blocks it the first time because the app is not signed with a paid Apple developer certificate: open System Settings → Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway". Pictures of each step are on https://homeapplabs.com/focus-energy/#install

Works on macOS 14 or later, on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.
